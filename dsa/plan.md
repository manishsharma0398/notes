# DSA — the 12-week plan

Mapped to `../STUDY-PLAN.md`. **40 minutes every weekday, 45 on weekend days. Never zero.**

Targets: **~25–30 problems by week 4 · ~60 by week 8 · ~90 by week 12.** Ninety understood beats
a hundred and fifty skimmed.

**Problem names are authoritative; the numbers are a convenience.** Search by name if a number
looks off.

## The weekly rhythm

| Day | What |
|---|---|
| Mon–Fri | 40 min. 1–2 problems from this week's pattern. Log each. |
| Sat | 45 min. A problem you failed earlier in the week, from scratch. |
| Sun | 45 min. One new problem + skim the week's log and write one line on what was weak. |

**Every Sunday, answer one question in the log:** *which pattern would I fail if asked tomorrow?*
That answer picks next week's Saturday problem.

## If a week slips

Do not skip the pattern to stay on schedule — the patterns build. Drop **problem count**, not
patterns: four problems from a pattern beats zero. The only thing that must not slip is doing
something every day.
