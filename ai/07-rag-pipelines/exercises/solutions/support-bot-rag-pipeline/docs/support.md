# Support & SLAs

## Response Times

Support response times vary by plan tier:

- **Free tier**: 72 hours (business days only)
- **Pro tier**: 24 hours (business days only)
- **Business tier**: 12 hours (business days only)
- **Enterprise**: 4 hours (24/7, including weekends and holidays)

Response time is measured from the time a ticket is submitted and confirmed received. Resolution time depends on issue complexity.

## Contact Channels

**Email Support**
All tiers: support@example.com

**Live Chat**
Available for Pro, Business, and Enterprise customers. Access via the chat icon in the bottom-right corner of the dashboard.

**Phone Support**
Available for Enterprise customers only. Your dedicated account manager's phone number is provided at onboarding.

**Enterprise Dedicated Slack Channel**
Enterprise customers receive a dedicated shared Slack channel with the support team at onboarding. This is the fastest way to reach the team for urgent issues.

## Submitting a Support Ticket

To submit a ticket:
1. Go to Help > Submit a Request from the dashboard
2. Select the issue category
3. Describe the problem and attach screenshots if applicable
4. Click Submit

You will receive a ticket number by email. Use this number to track your request.

## Incident & Outage Updates

Check status.example.com for real-time system status and incident reports. Subscribe to status updates to receive email or SMS alerts when incidents are opened or resolved.

## Feature Requests

Feature requests can be submitted via the community forum at community.example.com. Upvote existing requests or create new ones. The product team reviews top-voted requests quarterly.
