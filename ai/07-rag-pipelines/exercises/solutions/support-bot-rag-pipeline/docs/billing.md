# Billing & Payments

Monthly plans are billed on the same day each month. Annual plans are billed once per year at a discounted rate (2 months free compared to monthly billing).

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Cryptocurrency payments are not supported. Bank transfers (ACH) are available for Enterprise customers only.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails, we retry 3 times over 7 days:
- Retry 1: 2 days after the failed attempt
- Retry 2: 4 days after the failed attempt
- Retry 3: 7 days after the failed attempt

After 3 failed attempts, the account is downgraded to the Free tier. All data is retained for 30 days, giving you time to update your payment method and reactivate.

## Invoice Access

Invoices are available in Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History. You can download invoices as PDF for each billing cycle. Invoices are also emailed automatically to the billing email address on file.

## Changing Your Plan

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from Account Settings > Subscription. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## VAT & Tax

For customers in the EU, VAT is applied at the rate applicable to your country. You can add your VAT number in Account Settings > Billing to receive VAT-exempt invoices if eligible.

## Billing Contact

To update your billing email or address, go to Account Settings > Billing > Billing Information. For billing disputes, contact billing@example.com.
