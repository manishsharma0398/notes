# Onboarding Guide

## Getting Started

Welcome to the platform! This guide walks you through the first steps to get up and running.

## Step 1: Create Your Workspace

After signing up, you are prompted to create your first Workspace. A Workspace is a shared environment for your team. You can have multiple Workspaces under one account (e.g., one per client or project).

- Go to Dashboard > New Workspace
- Enter a name and optional description
- Invite team members by email

## Step 2: Invite Team Members

To invite team members to a Workspace:
1. Open the Workspace settings (gear icon, top right)
2. Click Members > Invite Member
3. Enter the email address and select a role:
   - **Owner**: Full admin access, can delete the workspace
   - **Admin**: Can manage members and settings, cannot delete workspace
   - **Member**: Can create and edit content, cannot manage members
   - **Viewer**: Read-only access

Invited members receive an email with a link valid for 48 hours. Expired invites can be resent from Workspace > Members > Pending Invites.

## Step 3: Connect Your Data Sources

Supported integrations at setup:
- Google Drive
- Notion
- Slack (read-only)
- GitHub
- CSV / file upload

Go to Workspace Settings > Integrations to connect your first source.

## Step 4: Set Up Notifications

Configure notifications at the account level (Account Settings > Notifications) or per-Workspace. You can receive notifications via email, Slack, or in-app.

## Step 5: Explore the Dashboard

The main dashboard shows:
- Recent activity feed
- Pinned projects
- Upcoming scheduled tasks
- Team member activity

Use the left sidebar to navigate between Workspaces, Projects, and Settings.

## Getting Help During Onboarding

Enterprise customers are assigned a dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) who will schedule an onboarding call within 24 hours of account creation. For all other tiers, the in-app onboarding checklist guides you through setup. You can also visit docs.example.com for full documentation.
