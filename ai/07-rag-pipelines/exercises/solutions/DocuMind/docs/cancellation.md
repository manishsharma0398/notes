# Cancellation Policy

## Monthly Subscribers

Monthly subscribers can cancel at any time from Account Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You will retain full access to all features until the period ends.

## Annual Subscribers

Annual subscribers who cancel within 14 days of their renewal date receive a full refund for the renewed term. After 14 days from renewal, no refund is issued and the subscription remains active until the end of the annual term.

If you purchased an annual plan for the first time (not a renewal), you have a 30-day money-back guarantee — no questions asked.

## How to Cancel

To cancel your subscription:
1. Go to Account Settings
2. Click Subscription
3. Click Cancel Plan
4. Select your reason (optional)
5. Confirm cancellation

You will receive a confirmation email immediately after cancellation.

## What Happens to Your Data After Cancellation

After cancellation, your account moves to the Free tier. Your data (projects, files, integrations) is retained for 90 days. After 90 days, data is permanently deleted. You can export your data at any time from Account Settings > Data Export.

## Reactivating a Cancelled Subscription

You can reactivate at any time before your data is deleted. Go to Account Settings > Subscription > Reactivate. All your previous data will be restored immediately.

## Pausing Instead of Cancelling

If you need a temporary break, you can pause your subscription for up to 3 months instead of cancelling. Go to Account Settings > Subscription > Pause Plan. Billing is suspended during the pause period.
